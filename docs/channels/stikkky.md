# stikkky - channel doctrine

Config: `channel-stikkky.toml`. Runs: `projects-stikkky/`. Account and upload state: [youtube-accounts.md](../youtube-accounts.md).

**Premise.** Anything animated stickman, no fixed subject. Renamed 2026-08-31 from Nature Is Trying,
which pinned the channel to one lane; the name commits to the look rather than the subject, so each
topic is picked on view data alone with no pillar to fill.

**Format.** Illustrative for ranked lists and countdowns, which is what every Run so far has been.
Narrative is available here and has not been used yet - the channel's look does not decide its
format.

**Intro.** Ten to fifteen seconds, not the thirty to forty a countdown channel usually takes. Two or
three Scenes: hook, then the ranking rule, then straight into the first card.

**The presenter set is per lane, not per channel.** The nature Runs share a naturalist study of
specimen jars and animal skulls, held as a Visual Bible entry. A topic off that lane gets its own
set rather than borrowing this one.

**Outro.** The channel sign-off, replacing Nature Is Trying's "Subscribe. It is still trying.":

> None of this was built for you. You are just the one who has to live with it.
> Subscribe. There is always another one.

The three finished Runs above the sleep Run still end on the old line and need two clips and a
re-render each, with no image redrawn.

**Cards.** Single large numerals, never a marked row of ten. That is what fixed the wrong-digit
problem which beat three attempts on channel one; two Runs in a row have come back correct first
time. Every card Scene carries `plain_card`, or eleven cards invent eleven different backgrounds.

**Clauses.** `people`, `creature`, `hallucination`, `plain_card`, plus the shared shot grammar. A
solid four-legged animal is a subject like any other here, unlike channel one.

**Avatar.** Not chosen. The candidates in `tuning/nature-avatar/` were drawn against the nature
framing and need redrawing for a general channel.
