# Could Be Worse - channel doctrine

Config: `channel.toml`. Runs: `projects/`. Account and upload state: [youtube-accounts.md](../youtube-accounts.md).

**Premise.** Grim things that happened to people, counted down. Every rank is damage done to a
person, which is what keeps a topic outside history inside the channel's register.

**Format.** Illustrative. The items in a countdown are genuinely unrelated, so no Scene promises
visual continuity with its neighbours.

**Intro.** Eight to ten Scenes, four to five seconds each, roughly twice the cutting rate of the
body. About half are the channel figure talking to camera, alternated with standalone metaphors
rather than grouped - four talking heads in a row read as one held frame, because a still does not
move and the viewer cannot tell the cut happened.

**The presenter set**, held as a Visual Bible entry and pasted verbatim into every presenter Scene:

> a warm dim records room with tall dark wooden shelves of stacked box files running back on both
> sides, a plain wooden floor, and one desk lamp glowing off to one side

It earns its place twice: richer than a flat background, and it is the literal claim the intro
makes, that these stories come out of the written record.

**No outro.** The video ends on its last ranked item. Removed 2026-09-08: the fixed turn and ask
landed unconnected to whatever the video had just been about, so they read as arriving out of
nowhere. The countdown's last item is its own ending.

The channel avatar still exists for the intro presenter Scenes; it is simply no longer the last
thing on screen. And the fifteen-second tail those three Scenes gave YouTube's end screen is gone
with them, so there is nowhere to put a subscribe button or a next-video card.

**Clauses.** `people` only, plus the shared shot grammar. No creature clause: this channel's stick
figure treatment is people, and the negative prompt bans stick figure animals outright.
